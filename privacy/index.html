Privacy and cookies

We collect almost nothing. Here is the almost.

This is the notice required by Articles 13 and 14 of the GDPR. It is short because the site is short: no account, no tracking, no advertising, and no third-party script that we have not put here on purpose.

What this site does

Four claims you can check in the source.

Everything below is verifiable from your own browser's network panel, which is the only kind of privacy claim worth making. If you find a request this page does not mention, that is a bug and we want to hear about it.

No cookies by default

The site sets no cookie of any kind. If you accept measurement, your choice is stored in your browser's local storage — not a cookie, and never sent anywhere.

Fonts served from here

The two typefaces are files on this domain. Nothing is fetched from Google Fonts, which would disclose your IP address to a US server before you had agreed to anything.

No forms, no form vendor

The briefing page composes a message in your own mail client. Nothing you type into it reaches us, or anyone else, until you press send in your own mail programme.

No advertising or social scripts

No pixels, no embedded video, no share buttons, no chat widget, no tag manager. There is nothing on this site whose job is to follow you off it.

Where this site is served

On a US host, for now. We would rather say it.

These pages are published through GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub, Inc. in the United States. Serving any page means GitHub processes your IP address and request metadata in order to deliver it and to defend the service against abuse. GitHub acts as our processor for that, under Standard Contractual Clauses and its Data Protection Agreement.

We are aware of the tension. A firm arguing that your files should not cross a border is currently serving its own marketing from Virginia. It affects visitors to a public website, not client material — no client file, document or model ever touches this host, and the kit we install has no connection to it. But it is a fair thing to hold against us, and moving the site to a European host is on the list rather than in a footnote.

01

Legal basis: legitimate interest in delivering and defending the site, Art. 6(1)(f).

02

We cannot read those logs. We hold no server-side record of your visit.

03

Client work never runs here. It runs inside the client's own boundary.

Cookies and measurement

Nothing loads before you have said yes.

We would like to know which pages are read, and we would like to know it without cookies and without a third party. The plan is a self-hosted counter on a European server, which records a page path and a coarse referrer and builds no profile and no cross-site identifier.

Until that is running, the site asks you nothing and measures nothing — which is why you may not have seen a banner. When it is running, the script is requested only after you accept, decline is a button of equal weight, and refusing leaves no storage behind at all.

Strictly necessary

One local-storage entry, radice.consent, holding the word granted or denied. It exists in order to honour your answer, and it is never transmitted.

Measurement

Loaded only on consent. No cookie, no fingerprint, no advertising identifier, and Do Not Track is respected even after you have accepted.

Everything else

There is no everything else. No marketing, personalisation or A/B testing category exists on this site to switch off.

Change your mind. Reopen the choice at any time, or clear this site's storage in your browser. Withdrawing consent has to be as easy as giving it, so it is one link in a sentence rather than a preference centre.

Email you send us

Ordinary correspondence, kept briefly.

If you write to us, we hold what you wrote: your name, your firm, your address, and whatever you chose to tell us. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering, or the steps before a contract where you have asked for a proposal.

We keep it while the conversation is live and delete it within twelve months of the last message, unless it has become a contract, in which case the retention that applies is the one in the contract. Our mail is handled by our email provider as a processor. Please do not send privileged or client-identifying material by email — we do not need it to answer.

Your rights

The full set, and how to use them.

You may ask for a copy of what we hold, ask us to correct or erase it, ask us to restrict how we use it, object to our use of it, or ask for it in a portable form. Write to privacy@radice.ai and we will answer within one month.

You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority in the country where you live or work. You do not have to raise it with us first, though we would rather you did — it is faster and we may simply be wrong.

Controller

Radice — the provider identified on the legal notice. Until that registration is complete, correspondence reaches the same people at the address above.

No automated decisions

Nothing on this site profiles you or makes a decision about you by automated means.

Children

This site is addressed to professional firms and public bodies. It is not directed at children and collects nothing knowingly from them.

Changes

If a claim on this page stops being true, the page changes the same day. There is no version of this document that is quietly out of date.
