02 — What remains

It stays.

Their machines, or a European room they control. Their document types — packs, invoices, extracts, checklists. Every answer can point at the file and the page. If it should not answer, it says so.

The stack

What is actually installed.

Not a platform. A runtime, one workflow that earns its place, and the paper a firm needs to keep both without us in the room.

Where it runs

Their workstation, office server, or a dedicated European tenant in their name.

First workflow

Pack search and a draft letter or checklist, on files they choose.

Handover

A runbook so the house can keep it, and a quiet monthly visit if they want us back without us becoming their IT department.

Done means

A partner uses it in the next close, and can say where a file went.

Limits

What the kit is not.

We do not replace the books system. The ledger stays where it is and the kit reads it — nobody should have to migrate a practice to get an answer out of their own filing cabinet.

We do not publish a time-saved percentage we have not measured. When there is a number worth quoting it will come from a named practice that agreed to be quoted.
