There is no form vendor behind this page. Pressing send hands the text to the mail programme on your own machine, addressed to us — so nothing you type here reaches a third party, and nothing reaches us until you send it yourself.

It is a slightly old-fashioned arrangement. It is also the only one we can describe honestly on a site that sells a boundary.

Please leave out client names and anything privileged. We do not need them to answer, and this message travels as ordinary email.
